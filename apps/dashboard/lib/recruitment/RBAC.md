# Recruitment RBAC

- HR Recruiter: create/manage requisitions, candidates, screening, interviews, offers in draft.
- HR Manager: validate/approve HR stages, review exceptions, manage recruitment configuration.
- Hiring Manager: confirm requisitions, panel participation, candidate recommendations.
- Department Manager/GM: approve manpower and department hiring.
- Finance/Budget: validate budget, salary affordability and controlled offer approvals.
- CFO: compensation/budget approvals according to threshold.
- MD/CEO: final/high-threshold approvals.
- Interview Panel: only assigned interview/evaluation records.
- Background Check Officer: assigned checks only.
- Auditor: read-only reports and audit.
- Admin/Super Admin: feature/RBAC configuration, not automatic access to candidate sensitive documents.
